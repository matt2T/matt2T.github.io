Mattrocious

Say friend are you down on your luck? Alone? Feeling like the people in your life may be demons? Have you ever thought what if I could just get hit by a semi-truck and reincarnate to an overpowered adventurer with a harem of young kick ass women who may or may not have a tail? Then you need to get help...but you won't find any here!

Oh well...but anime, as loopy and twisted as it is, still better than the slop on streaming and network TV.

So if you've just given up on your ambitions now that AI has come along and the American dream seems out of reach, lets just watch Japanese cartoons. Take THAT, adulting! You can have your fancy mortgage, your high income tax brackets and your 401(k).
